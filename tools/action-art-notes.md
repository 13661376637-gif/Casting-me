Background brief: a new 16:10 snow-night courtyard based on the supplied live-action reference, tiled garden wall, snow-covered pines and shrubs, open central snow floor, cinema camera on the far left and film lamp on the far right. Western visual-novel ink linework, muted flat navy/sage/cream painted shapes, subtle paper grain; no people, lettering, glossy lighting or UI.

Second pose brief: the same black/red/gold qipao heroine as the supplied character reference, composed low guard with sword raised diagonally to viewer left and closed scabbard lowered to viewer right. Preserve identity, costume, adult proportions, clear hand grips, connected shoulders/wrists, continuous blade/guard/handle axis, all weapon tips in frame. Flat inked hand-drawn game style.

The generated second-pose reference had a baked neutral checker; the offline contour importer excludes its exterior checker pixels. This is not used as a game texture. Both original and generated reference files remain unchanged.
